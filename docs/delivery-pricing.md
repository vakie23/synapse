# Delivery Pricing Rules

## Regional Zones
- Zone A (major cities): base fee 4.00
- Zone B (secondary cities): base fee 7.50
- Zone C (remote): base fee 12.00

## Weight Bands
- 0-2kg: +0.00
- 2-10kg: +3.00
- >10kg: +8.00

## Service Level
- Standard delivery: included in base fee.
- Express delivery: +5.00 (where available).

## Formula
`delivery_fee = zone_base + weight_band + service_surcharge`

## Payment Constraints
- COD is available for Zone A and Zone B.
- Zone C requires online payment for first-time customers.
